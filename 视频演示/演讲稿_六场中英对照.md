# 六场故障演示 · 中英对照演讲稿（Bilingual）

> 总时长 10 分钟，共六段，每段独立、对应一个 Streamlit 页面。
> 语言：中文为主，英文逐句对照；词汇控制在雅思 6.5 左右，只保留必要专业词。
> 每段开头是"操作提示"（屏幕上点哪），然后是"逐句口播"。
> 六段讲完有一句收口。数字全部来自官方 CSV 与 `src/sim/` 仿真，可当场复算。

---

## 开场 · Opening（30 秒，讲完再进第一场）

**中文**
"各位好。今天我们用六个画面，讲六个真实发生在 11 月 13 号那天的故障。
每一个画面只做一件事：先看——如果还是用原来的系统，这个故障会带来什么；
再看——用我们这套系统，同样一个故障会变成什么。
左右两边是同一批车、同一个时刻，差别只来自我们的一个决定。
我们不吹设备永不出故障，我们讲的是：故障来了，不用人动手。"

**English**
"Good afternoon. Over the next ten minutes, we will show you six events that really
happened on November 13th. Each screen does one thing: first, what the old system
would do; then, what ours does. Same vehicles, same moment — the only difference is
one decision we made. We are not claiming our machines never fail. We are saying:
when something fails, no human has to step in."

## 第 1 场 · 机柜过热停机（约 2 分钟）

> **页面**：`1_Night_Replay.py`
> **操作**：点 **Restart**，再点 **Jump to → Fault**（02:25）。讲完可点 **Morning** 看天亮结账。

**中文（逐句）**
"S1 是一个公交场站。夜里回来 24 辆车，但只有 12 把枪。
枪只有车的一半，所以到了半夜，必须有人起来——把充满的车拔掉，插到下一辆车上。
凌晨 00:30，这个人一共走了 9 趟，每一趟 8 分钟。
半夜两点二十五分，一个充电柜过热停机了。
原来的系统里，这个柜下面 4 把枪全死。枪还插在车上，凌晨没人来拔，这 4 辆车就挂在死柜上，等到天亮也没电。
我们的做法，是把 24 个充电点全部接到一条共用的母排上。
柜子死了，电就从另外两个柜绕过来。枪没死，只是总功率从 1080 千瓦掉到 720 千瓦。
天亮了算账：原来 4 辆车欠电发车、9 次人工；我们这边，0 辆欠电、0 次人工。
省人力不是因为我们做得快，是因为那个'半夜拔枪'的动作，根本没有理由发生了。"

**English**
"S1 is a bus depot. Twenty-four buses come back at night, but there are only twelve
guns. So in the middle of the night, a person has to get up and unplug a full bus to
plug in the next one. At 00:30 this person made nine trips.
At 02:25, one charging cabinet overheated and shut down.
In the old system, all four guns under that cabinet died. The cables were still
plugged in, no one came to unplug them, so those four buses sat on a dead cabinet
until morning.
Our approach: all twenty-four points connect to one shared busbar. When a cabinet
dies, power routes around it through the other two. The guns stay alive — only total
power drops from 1,080 to 720 kilowatts.
Morning tally: the old system has four buses short and nine human actions. Ours has
zero and zero.
We save labor not because we are faster, but because that midnight unplugging no
longer has a reason to exist."

## 第 2 场 · 断网（约 2 分钟）

> **页面**：`2_Outage_Replay.py`
> **操作**：点 **Jump to → Outage 11:00**，再点 **Mid-outage 12:00**，最后 **Restored 13:00**。

**中文（逐句）**
"这一场只发生一件事：11 点到 13 点，这个高速站断网了两个小时。
注意，断的不是电，断的是'谁来决定每把枪给多少功率'。
原来的设计里，这个决定是在云端算好、再发下来的。云一断，场站不敢自己算——怕算错烧了变压器——于是退回最保险的做法：每把枪固定 50 千瓦。
10 把枪，全站从 1425 千瓦掉到 500 千瓦。
一辆货车要充 224 度电，正常 45 分钟；现在变成 269 分钟。这些司机在数据里最多只肯等 46 分钟，所以后面的人一看排队，掉头就走。
我们的做法，是在场站里放一个本地的大脑。云断了，它自己接着算'谁要多少、总共不超 1500 千瓦'。
这件事从来就不需要云。所以同样的断网、同样的车，右边的 10 把枪照常充电，司机根本感觉不到断过网。
这一场的结论是：断网真正断掉的是决策权，不是电。把决策权放回场站，断网就只是一条通知。"

**English**
"This scene has one event: from 11:00 to 13:00, this highway hub lost its network
for two hours.
Notice — it lost the connection, not the power. What it lost was the answer to 'who
decides how much power each gun gives'.
In the old design, that decision is made in the cloud and sent down. When the cloud
is gone, the station is afraid to decide on its own — afraid of burning the
transformer — so it falls back to the safest setting: 50 kilowatts per gun.
Ten guns: the whole station drops from 1,425 to 500 kilowatts.
A truck needs 224 kilowatt-hours. Normally that is 45 minutes; now it takes 269.
In the data, these drivers wait at most 46 minutes. So people see the queue and
drive away.
Our approach: we put a local brain inside the station. When the cloud is gone, it
keeps deciding on its own — who needs what, never above 1,500 kilowatts. This never
needed the cloud in the first place. So with the same outage and the same trucks,
the ten guns on our side keep charging. The drivers never notice.
The point: an outage takes away the decision, not the power. Move the decision back
into the station, and an outage is just a notification."

## 第 3 场 · 电价尖峰（约 1.5 分钟）

> **页面**：`3_Price_Spike_Replay.py`
> **操作**：点 **Jump to → Peak**（18:00），看价格曲线冲到最高点。

**中文（逐句）**
"这一场没有设备坏，也没有限电，只有一件事：电价变了。
17 点 15 到 18 点 45，电价从 69 欧涨到 799 欧每兆瓦时，涨了十倍多。
原来的做法是'谁插着谁就充'，8 辆货车一起分 800 千瓦。
结果有一辆车 T02，17 点 45 必须发车，功率却被平均分掉了，最后差一点点，欠电发车。这一夜电费 342 欧。
我们只改了一件事：先看发车时间，再看电价。
T01 到 T04 必须走的，先喂满；T05 到 T08 明天早上才走，最贵的这 90 分钟先等一等，等价格掉下来再补。
结果 8 辆车全满，电费 179 欧，省了将近一半。
关键要说清楚：这不是'为了省钱不让车充电'。急着走的车一直在充；被推迟的，只有'明天才走'的车。
我们的顺序永远是：安全第一，电网约束第二，发车时限第三，电价最后。"

**English**
"This scene has no broken machine and no power cut. Only one thing: the price.
From 17:15 to 18:45, the price went from 69 to 799 euros per megawatt-hour — more
than ten times higher.
The old habit is 'whoever is plugged in keeps charging', so eight trucks share
800 kilowatts evenly. One truck, T02, has to leave at 17:45, but its power was
spread out, so it left a little short. That night cost 342 euros.
We changed one thing: look at the departure time first, then the price.
T01 to T04 must leave, so we fill them first. T05 to T08 leave tomorrow morning, so
they wait through the most expensive 90 minutes and charge when the price drops.
Result: all eight trucks full, at 179 euros — nearly half off.
To be clear: this is not 'saving money by not charging'. The trucks that must leave
keep charging. Only the 'leave tomorrow' trucks wait.
Our order never changes: safety first, then the grid limit, then the departure time,
and the price comes last."

## 第 4 场 · 电网限电（约 1.5 分钟）

> **页面**：`4_Curtailment_Replay.py`
> **操作**：点 **Jump to → Cap**（16:00），再点 **Cap ends**（19:00）。

**中文（逐句）**
"这一场也没有价格，只有电网的一句话：16 点到 19 点，这个物流站被限到 400 千瓦，原来有 800 千瓦。
原来的做法是：先照顾已经在充的过夜货车，因为它们插得早、功率大。
结果 400 千瓦大部分被这些过夜车占着，17 点半、17 点 45、18 点、18 点 15 要走的四辆车，反而分不到电。
最后 4 辆车全部欠电。
我们做的，还是同一个 400 千瓦，只改分配规则：谁先走，谁先充。
限电窗口里，要发车的车优先；过夜车先让一让。19 点限电一解除，过夜车再用 800 千瓦补回来。
最后 8 辆车全满，一辆都不少。
这一场想说的是：限电不是'少给电'的问题，是'少给谁'的问题。同样的 400 千瓦，排对顺序，就一个都不少。"

**English**
"No price here either. Just one message from the grid: from 16:00 to 19:00, this
logistics depot is cut to 400 kilowatts, from 800.
The old habit: look after the overnight trucks first, because they plugged in early
and draw a lot. So those overnight trucks hold most of the 400 kilowatts, and the
four trucks that must leave at 17:30, 17:45, 18:00 and 18:15 get almost nothing.
All four leave short.
We use the same 400 kilowatts and change only the rule: whoever leaves first, charges
first. During the cut, the departing trucks go first; the overnight trucks wait.
At 19:00 the cap lifts and the overnight trucks catch up at 800 kilowatts.
Result: all eight full, not one short.
The point: a power cut is not 'less power' — it is 'less power for whom'. Sort the
same 400 kilowatts in the right order, and nobody misses out."

## 第 5 场 · 电缆损坏（约 1 分钟）

> **页面**：`5_Cable_Fault_Replay.py`
> **操作**：点 **Jump to → Fault**（21:30），看 D4-2 变红。

**中文（逐句）**
"这一场只坏一把枪：21 点 30，城市站里一条充电电缆损坏了。
原来的流程是：故障先上报，等云端确认，再等人工开票——大约 30 秒。
我们的做法是：本地的安全回路在 40 毫秒里就检测到损坏、断开电源，同时自动开一张维修工单，另外 7 把枪照常工作。
我要诚实地说：这个时刻车站没有满负荷，所以两边的充电量其实一样。
这一场比的是响应速度和恢复流程——30 秒变成 40 毫秒，意味着不再需要一个人盯着屏幕等告警、再手动派单。"

**English**
"This scene breaks only one gun: at 21:30, a charging cable in the city hub failed.
The old flow: report the fault, wait for cloud confirmation, then wait for a human
to open a ticket — about 30 seconds.
Ours: a local safety circuit detects the damage in 40 milliseconds, cuts the power,
opens a service ticket automatically, and the other seven guns keep working.
I want to be honest: at that moment the hub was not full, so the two sides charged
the same amount.
This scene is about response time and the recovery flow — thirty seconds becomes
forty milliseconds, which means no one has to watch the screen for an alarm and
then dispatch it by hand."

## 第 6 场 · 新站配置错误（约 1.5 分钟）

> **页面**：`6_Commissioning_Replay.py`
> **操作**：点 **Jump to → Mismatch**（00:00），再点 **Unlock**（03:30）。

**中文（逐句）**
"这一场最冤枉：设备全是好的，是参数写错了。
一个新公交站，两张表里写的是 900 千瓦，但第三张表把上限写成了 600 千瓦；
还有一把枪被限到 50 千瓦，另一把的通信卡没激活。
按这个错误配置跑，12 辆车里 5 辆欠电。
我们的做法是做一个三方配置对账器，把三张表放在一起对账。
它发现 600 对不上 900，自动改回 900；那把 50 千瓦的枪改回 150；没激活的通信卡激活。
同一批车、同一批枪，最后 0 辆欠电。
这一场的结论是：自运行不只是'出故障时自救'，还要'配置错了能自己发现'。
新站投运最怕的不是设备坏，是几张参数表互相打架。"

**English**
"This scene is the most unfair one: the machines are all fine — the numbers are
wrong. For a new depot, two tables say 900 kilowatts, but the third table says the
limit is 600. One gun is capped at 50 kilowatts, and another gun's SIM is not even
activated. Run on this wrong setting, five of the twelve buses leave short.
Our approach is a three-way config reconciler that lines up the three tables and checks them
against each other. It sees 600 does not match 900, and fixes it back to 900. The
50-kilowatt gun goes back to 150. The inactive SIM is activated.
Same buses, same guns — zero short.
The point: self-operating is not only 'save yourself when a fault hits'; it also
means 'find your own mistakes in the settings'. The scariest thing when a new site
opens is not broken hardware — it is tables that contradict each other."

## 收口 · Closing（30 秒，六场讲完）

**中文**
"六个画面看下来，你会发现我们做的不是六个补丁，而是同一套东西：
有车要充，先问它几点必须走；有故障，先问哪部分还活着、怎么绕过去；
有高价电，就问谁可以等；有坏枪，先保证人安全；有错参数，让系统自己对账。
这套系统的目标，不是让设备永不故障，而是让故障不再需要人来处理。
这就是我们说的自运行。谢谢。"

**English**
"Looking back at the six screens, we did not build six patches — we built one thing.
When a vehicle needs power, ask when it must leave. When something fails, ask what
is still alive and how to route around it. When power is expensive, ask who can
wait. When a gun breaks, keep people safe first. When a setting is wrong, let the
system check itself.
Our goal is not machines that never fail. It is failures that no longer need a
human. That is what we mean by self-operating. Thank you."
