# Public sources

The dataset is synthetic. For real numbers, start here:

## Electricity market and grid (Finland)
- **Fingrid open data**: consumption, production, wind, frequency and reserve market data. https://data.fingrid.fi/en/
- **Fingrid reserve markets** (FCR, aFRR, mFRR) and how flexible loads can take part: https://www.fingrid.fi/en/electricity-market/reserves-and-balancing/
- **ENTSO-E Transparency Platform**: day-ahead prices for bidding zone FI. https://transparency.entsoe.eu/
- **Nord Pool**: day-ahead and intraday market data. Since 2025 the day-ahead market uses 15-minute products. https://data.nordpoolgroup.com/

## Traffic
- **Fintraffic Digitraffic, road traffic**: TMS/LAM traffic measurement stations (vehicle counts by class). https://www.digitraffic.fi/en/road-traffic/
  The challenge data has two weeks of generated history shaped like TMS counts. Digitraffic is best for checking real patterns on a real road.

## Charging technology
- **Kempower products** (public product pages and datasheets): https://kempower.com/
- **Kempower documentation**: https://docs.kempower.io/
- **OCPP** (Open Charge Point Protocol, the common standard between chargers and backends): https://openchargealliance.org/

## Vehicles
- Manufacturer spec sheets for electric city buses and trucks give battery sizes and maximum charging power.
- Winter consumption of electric buses is typically 20–40 % higher than in summer.
