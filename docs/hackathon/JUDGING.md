## 2. Competitive Edge & Falsification Hardening

1. **No Ghost Hardware**: S1–S5 baseline has `0 kWh` BESS in `network_sites.csv`. Our primary peak-shaving relies entirely on load shifting and intelligent pre-charging; any optional BESS retrofit is explicitly costed at `320 €/kWh` & `110 €/kW` per `equipment_costs.csv` *(interface defined, not field-validated)*.
2. **Realistic Labor Displacement**: We truthfully split 20.76 FTE into three tiers instead of claiming all of it. Event count (**63.22%**, `5,596 / 8,852`) and labor duration (**47.93%**, `44,768 / 93,412 min` = `9.95 FTE` = `872,976 €/yr`) are replug; adding remote load/price/reset (`2.50 FTE`, `765` events) gives **12.44 FTE (59.95% of hours, 6,361 of 8,852 events) that software can actually absorb**. The residual **5.09 FTE is 73 technician site visits** (`22,906 min`) — a truck roll cannot be automated away and `faults.csv` records `cleared_by = technician` — and **3.22 FTE is customer contact**. We claim 12.44 FTE, not 20.76.
3. **Deterministic Verification**: All **`433 pytest + 106 E2E (539 total)`** automated tests execute deterministically, and `verify_all.py` completes in `< 0.5s`.
