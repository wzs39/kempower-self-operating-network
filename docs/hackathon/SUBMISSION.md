# Submission Package · Kempower Self-Operating Charging Network

## 1. Inspiration (Empirical Dataset Findings)
Across the 100-site European charging network (`240,635` rows across 45 official CSV files), operators spend **93,412 staff-minutes per two weeks (`1,556.87 h = 20.76 FTE`)** on manual interventions (`€1,821,534/yr` at the official `45 €/h = 87,750 €/FTE·yr` rate), of which **`move_and_replug_vehicle` accounts for 5,596 actions (63.22% by count) and 44,768 minutes (47.93% of work time = 9.95 FTE = €872,976/yr)**. On Crisis Friday `2026-11-13`, 13 fleet vehicles miss departure (`1,804.45 kWh` total deficit across S1, S2, and S5) while S3 suffers a `29.69%` public abandonment spike (`14.36%` 14-day baseline).

## 2. What It Does
**Project Sentinel** replaces reactive human operations with a two-tier autonomous operating system combining a **15-minute HiGHS MILP Macro-Scheduler** and a **millisecond Edge Micro-FSM**:
- **S1 Bus Depot (Three-Tier Resolution: `4` baseline -> `3` [`0 €` software] -> `0` [`324,000 €` expansion **+ unpriced busbar tie**])**: Detects `D1-9` (`C1-3`) thermal anomaly at `2026-11-12T22:31:00+02:00` (`59.3°C / 702 rpm`, `3h 19m` before `F015`). Under **Tier 2 (`0 €` CAPEX software urgency, 0 human moves)** on the existing 12 single-gun posts (`cross_tie=False`), missed departures drop from `4` (`B04, B09, B18, B20`) to **`3` (`B04, B09, B18` — `B20` saved)** and simulated energy deficit drops from **`398.5 kWh` to `48.8 kWh` (`-87.8%`)**. **Tier 3 requires two things, not one**: 12 extra dispensers (`324,000 €`, `13.31-yr` standalone payback) **plus** a cross-cabinet busbar tie. Dispensers alone make S1 **worse** (`8` missed, `1,040.1 kWh`, Cell 7 of `docs/attribution_matrix.md`), because cabinet power stays `1,080 kW` and each gun drops `90 -> 45 kW`. Cabinet upsizing was also tested: `3×450 kW` reaches `0` but peaks at `1,350 kW`, **over the `1,200 kW` grid limit**. **The only zero-miss configuration inside the grid limit is the busbar tie on the existing 12 guns (peak stays `1,080 kW`), and it has no price in `equipment_costs.csv`** — a stated capability boundary, not a claimed result.
- **S2 Logistics Depot (`4 -> 0` missed, `283.50 kWh` recovered)**: Preempts overnight trucks `T05–T08` (`1,857.06 kWh` afternoon pool) using 1 kW continuous OCPP setpoints during `14:20–18:15` so afternoon trucks `T01–T04` depart 100% on time despite the `400 kW` `G1` DSO curtailment (`33.3 kWh` raw grid headroom).
- **S5 Bus Depot (`5 -> 0` missed, `508.95 kWh` recovered)**: Three-Way Config Reconciler detects `site_config.csv` row 164 capping `grid_limit_kw` at `600 kW` (vs `900 kW` in `network_sites.csv` and `power_cabinets.csv`), restoring `900 kW` (`+750 kWh` in `03:30–06:00`), `D5-7` (`50 -> 150 kW`), and `D5-8` (`sim_active -> True`).
